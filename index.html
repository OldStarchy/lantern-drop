How to play

Fire a moonstone from the launcher at the top. Every lantern it touches lights up and is cleared when the shot ends. Light all the amber lanterns to win the level.

Jade lanterns trigger a power. Violet lanterns are worth 500 and move every turn. The more amber you light, the higher the score multiplier climbs.

Land the moonstone in the drifting boat for a free ball. Light the last amber lantern for the Grand Finale, where the stone drops into one of five prize lanterns.

Modes

Campaign has five chapters of five levels with classic rules. Before each level you pick one of your unlocked powers. Clearing a chapter unlocks two more. Earn up to three stars per level by finishing with balls to spare.

Free play lets you play any level with your own rules: infinite balls, more jade lanterns, any mix of powers.

Hot-seat match is for 2 to 4 players taking turns on one board. Each player has their own score, balls and powers. Highest score wins.

Destiny Shot takes a moment to think, then shows how much better its angle is than yours.

Controller

Left stick aims, right stick or bumpers fine-tune, A fires, hold Y to fast-forward, X mutes, Menu or B pauses.

Keyboard

← → aim (hold Shift to fine-tune), Space fires, hold F to fast-forward, M mutes, Esc pauses.

Mouse and touch

Point to aim and click to fire. On touch, drag to aim and lift your finger to fire. Lift above the launcher to cancel.

Free play rules

Paused

Press M or controller X during play to mute. Rule changes to lantern counts apply when a level starts.
